Gamma: price, catch and who it suits

Turns a prompt or rough notes into a deck, document or webpage draft. Output still needs a human pass for high-stakes decks.

The Weekly 2% Edge looks for small, repeatable gains in how a business runs. This page summarises our research on Gamma, read from the vendor’s own pages and from what other buyers report. It is not a hands-on test.

What it costs

Free tier; paid from €8 per seat a month billed annually (€10 monthly). Read on Gamma’s own pricing page on 2026-08-26; check the live page before you buy.

The credit model means heavy generation eats your monthly allowance, and outputs still need a human polish for high-stakes decks — it's a brilliant first-draft engine, not a finished-deck button. The free tier's watermark pushes you to paid quickly.

Who it suits

Anyone who wants to turn a prompt or notes into a good-looking presentation, doc or webpage draft in minutes.

Who should skip it

Skip it if you need pixel-perfect, brand-controlled decks — you'll spend the saved time re-polishing the AI output.

Getting started

The shortest route to a usable result starts on the Gamma dashboard: click Create new AI and choose Generate, Paste or Import. Generate takes a topic prompt, Paste reformats text written elsewhere, and Import accepts a PowerPoint, a document or a URL. Pasting existing text tends to reach something presentable fastest, since the model is styling material instead of inventing it. The common stumbling block is the free workspace, which caps a single prompt at 10 slides where paid workspaces allow up to 75, so long outlines quietly arrive truncated. Refinement happens per card through the sparkle icon at the top-left of a slide.
