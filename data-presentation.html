Browse AI & Beautiful.ai: From Raw Data to Board Deck

Intelligence gathering is manual. Presentation design is a tax.

Acquiring competitive intelligence—monitoring pricing changes, scraping lead lists, or tracking market shifts—usually requires hours of manual data entry or expensive custom scripts. Once you have that data, you face the "design tax": spending three hours fighting PowerPoint templates just to make the numbers look acceptable for a client or board meeting.

The Solution: The Data-to-Deck Pipeline

By chaining two specific tools together, you can automate this entire pipeline.

First, use Browse AI to eliminate manual scraping. You record your screen once to show the AI what data to look for, and it continuously extracts the structured intelligence directly into a spreadsheet, requiring zero code.

Second, feed those insights into Beautiful.ai. It is an AI presentation maker built for work output, not toy slides. The AI does the design thinking: you describe the point of the slide, and it automatically balances the layout, builds charts from your data, and locks in brand colors.

Key Efficiencies & ROI

  • Eliminate the Design Tax: Beautiful.ai drops the average time spent on a client deck from 3 hours to just 25 minutes.
  • Automated Competitor Tracking: Browse AI turns any website into a structured API, allowing you to track market changes in real-time without developer resources.
  • Brand Consistency at Scale: Beautiful.ai's smart templates prevent off-brand, amateur-looking slides. Edits re-flow automatically; you never have to drag text boxes again.
