Browse.ai and Beautiful.ai: prices, catches and who they suit

The Weekly 2% Edge looks for small, repeatable gains in how a business runs. This page summarises our research on Browse.ai and Beautiful.ai, read from the vendors’ own pages and from what other buyers report. It is not a hands-on test.

Browse.ai

No-code web scraping and data extraction. Turn any website into an automated data pipeline or spreadsheet automatically.

What it costs

Free plan; Personal $19 a month billed annually (12,000 credits/yr), or $48/mo billed monthly. Read on Browse.ai’s own pricing page on 2026-09-21; check the live page before you buy.

Two honest realities of no-code scraping. Credits burn faster than expected: row counts, screenshots and 'premium' sites all multiply the meter, so monitor a big site daily and do the math first. And scrapers break when sites redesign — robots need maintenance, which is your time even when the tool is no-code. Also check the terms of the sites you monitor; 'technically possible' and 'permitted' aren't the same thing.

Who it suits

Ops, research and growth teams that want to monitor competitors, prices or listings and pipe structured web data into sheets without writing scrapers.

Who should skip it

Skip it if you need one-off extractions (free tools or a VA are cheaper), or mission-critical pipelines where you'd rather own the code than rent the robot.

Getting started

The documented route is short. From the dashboard you click Build New Robot, choose extraction or monitoring, enter the Origin URL, and click Start Training Robot. Robot Studio loads the live page and you point and click to capture data 'From a list' or as 'Just text', or take screenshots. You then set pagination, label columns, finish recording and approve the sample. Browse.ai says this takes under 10 minutes (their claim, not our measurement).

Beautiful.ai

Presentation builder whose templates lay themselves out as you type.

What it costs

From $14.50/mo billed annually (a one-off month is $45). Read on Beautiful.ai’s own pricing page on 2026-09-02; check the live page before you buy.

The smart-template engine is the feature and the fence: decks look polished with zero design skill precisely because the system constrains you, so pixel-perfect custom layouts and heavy brand exceptions fight the tool. And note the billing gap — $14.50/mo annual becomes $45 month-to-month, one of the steeper monthly premiums around. If your organisation lives in PowerPoint workflows, test the import/export round-trip before committing.

Who it suits

Founders, sales and small teams that want consistently good-looking decks fast, where design consistency matters more than pixel-level control.

Who should skip it

Skip it if you need intricate custom layouts or complex animations (designers want full control), or your whole company workflow is deeply PowerPoint-native.

Getting started

The vendor route to a first deck is a four-stage flow: click Create Presentation, pick Create With AI, then enter or paste a prompt, optionally attaching documents, PDFs or links. From there you edit the generated text-only outline, set visual preferences such as theme, image source and language, and click Generate Presentation. Slide AI handles per-slide regeneration afterwards. The friction sits around that flow, not in it. The 14-day trial requires a debit or credit card up front and rolls into a paid subscription when it expires, and cancelling requires a desktop or laptop.
