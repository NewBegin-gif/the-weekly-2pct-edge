I Abandoned PowerPoint for Beautiful.ai — Here's My Honest Founder Review

If you've ever spent a whole evening nudging text boxes around in PowerPoint, you'll know exactly the feeling I'm talking about. After more than two decades in international banking, I lost count of the hours I burned lining up boxes, fixing charts that kept breaking, and chasing bullet points that wouldn't behave.

So earlier this year, when I sat down on my balcony on the 35th floor here in Ho Chi Minh City to map out the pitch for my new AI-driven SEO project, I knew I didn't want to go through all that again. I needed a sharp, good-looking slide deck, but I flat-out refused to lose days of my life to the old grind. I wanted something that understood I'm a founder with ideas, not a designer. That's what led me to Beautiful.ai.

What Beautiful.ai actually is, in plain words

Put simply, Beautiful.ai is a presentation tool that takes care of all the design and layout for you. Where PowerPoint or Google Slides drop you in front of a blank white page and total freedom (which usually ends up looking like a mess), Beautiful.ai quietly keeps everything tidy for you in the background.

Smart Slides: no more nudging boxes around

The heart of Beautiful.ai — and the reason I won't go back to the old tools — is what they call Smart Slides. Picture a slide with three neat columns. At the last second you decide to add a fourth point. In PowerPoint that kicks off that maddening little dance. In Beautiful.ai you click "add column" and start typing. It quietly resizes and re-balances the other three for you, right there on screen.

Creating with AI: shaping the story first

  1. Give it something to work with. Type a prompt or drop in a file — a PDF, or a rough outline you put together in ChatGPT.
  2. See the outline first. It shows you a plain text outline before anything is designed.
  3. Add your brand. Bring in your own colours, logo and fonts.
  4. Build and tidy up. It turns the outline into finished slides, and from there you can just ask for tweaks per slide.

How It Reclaimed My Time

Beautiful.ai takes over all the annoying design work, so we can throw together a gorgeous presentation in just a few minutes. It saves us a ton of screen-time, and it leaves us with plenty of time to close our laptops and enjoy the day together.
