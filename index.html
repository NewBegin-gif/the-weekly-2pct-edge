02 Open exposure

Realized vs. open: the part most bot results leave out

Every weekly figure above is realized: profit that was locked in by selling. The bitcoin the bot still holds is open, and it is not in those figures.

In the weekly figure

Realized

A position counts the moment it is sold, in the week of the sale.

  • Fees on the buy and the sell are already taken off.
  • The rare forced exits at a loss are included.
  • Once it's in, it doesn't change.
Not in the weekly figure

Open

Bitcoin bought but not yet sold. Its value moves with the price, every minute.

  • Nothing is locked in until it's sold.
  • A falling price shows up here first.
  • It reaches the weekly figure only when sold.

03 Measured leaks

Where the edge leaks

A bot that only ever sells at a profit still loses money in quieter ways. These are the leaks I measured on this bot, each with the date it was measured or fixed.

Leak 1measured 5 Aug 2026; fee tiers read 8 Sep 2026

~20%

of gross profit went to fees

Exchange fees took about a fifth of gross profit

Between late April and early August, fees came to roughly 20% of gross trading profit. The bot now books the real fee per fill. The exchange charges 0.18% per taker order and 0.09% per maker order at the bot's volume.

Real fee now booked per fill

Leak 2measured 5 Aug 2026

0.33%

given up per trade

Lowering the profit target over time gave money away

A 'time-decay' rule lowered the sell target as a position aged. Of 152 sales it touched over 55 days, the original target was still reached in 78% of cases (median: 3 hours later). The rule gave up about 0.33% per trade, so it was switched off.

Rule switched off

Leak 3changed 9 Aug 2026

RSI 70

hard block that froze the ladder

A safety filter froze the buy ladder

A hard block on buying when RSI was above 70 also stopped the ladder from following the price, so a later shallow dip caught nothing. It became a taper: smaller orders between RSI 70 and 80, a hard block only above 80.

Now a taper; hard block only above 80

Leak 4fixed 9 Aug 2026

~½

of one week's realized profit missing from the report

One weekly report missed about half of that week's profit

In one week of the bot's Telegram weekly report, about half of the realized profit was missing. The report filtered on when a position started instead of when it was sold, so positions spanning two weeks disappeared. Fixed, and checked against the trade records.

Fixed and checked against trade records

05 Method

The rules of this logbook

Every figure comes from the bot's own records through its read-only dashboard, the same numbers I look at. A script builds the weekly series and summaries from the dashboard; the dated leak measurements and reference goals are maintained separately. How each number is computed.

  • Percentages only. No amounts, no account size, no order details.
  • Weeks one week delayed. A week's result appears here seven days after it ends. The open-position range and the strategy table are read on the build date.
  • Fees on both sides. Every figure is after the exchange fee on the buy and on the sell.
  • Loss sales count. The rare forced exits at a loss are in the weekly figures.
  • Open positions shown next to it. Realized profit is never presented as the whole picture.
  • No signals, no advice, no copy-trading. This is a logbook, not a service.
