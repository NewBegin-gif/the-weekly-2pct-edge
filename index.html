Building workflows to reclaim our time.

Time is the only non-renewable asset. After two decades navigating the high-pressure world of international finance, I traded the daily grind for a life defined by freedom. Now, back in Rotterdam after years in Asia, I build quiet, autonomous systems that handle the heavy digital lifting.

By optimizing operations by just 2% every week, I create the space to step away from the screen, spend more meaningful time with my wife, and guide our son and daughter as they grow.

Below are research summaries of business tools: what each one does, the price we read on the vendor’s own page and the date we read it, and the catch. None of them is a hands-on test. The tools I run myself are a Hostinger VPS, Replit and Bitvavo, and I write about those on The Weekly AI Edge. Some links are affiliate links: if you sign up, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
How we read a tool
1. The price comes from the vendor’s own pricing page, with the date we read it.
2. The catch: the condition that changes the bill, from the terms and from what buyers report.
3. Who it suits and who should skip it.
4. A link to the full review with every source.

We do not claim to use a tool unless we do.
