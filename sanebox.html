SaneBox: Automating Email Triage to Reclaim 4 Hours a Day

The core problem with email is not volume; it is context-switching.

The average professional spends over four hours a day managing their inbox, making it the most time-consuming work activity facing the workforce today. The math is brutal: it takes 64 seconds to recover from an email interruption. Worse, internal data shows that 58% of the emails you receive are completely unimportant. You are spending your most valuable asset—time—sorting through noise.

The Solution: Algorithmic Triage

SaneBox is not a new email client. It is an AI layer that sits on top of your existing infrastructure (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud) and acts as a ruthless gatekeeper. It does not read your email content; it analyzes the headers and your past interaction behavior to determine importance. If an email is not critical, SaneBox intercepts it before it hits your inbox.

Key Efficiencies & ROI

  • Zero Context-Switching: By filtering out the 58% of irrelevant emails, your inbox only contains actionable items.
  • Automated Follow-ups: SaneBox’s Reply Tracking automatically notifies you if a sent email goes unanswered.
  • One-Click Unsubscribe: Move an email to the @SaneBlackHole folder, and SaneBox sends all future emails from that sender straight to the trash.
