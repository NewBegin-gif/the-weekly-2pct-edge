Melio and TaxCycle: prices, catches and who they suit

The Weekly 2% Edge looks for small, repeatable gains in how a business runs. This page summarises our research on Melio and TaxCycle, read from the vendors’ own pages and from what other buyers report. It is not a hands-on test.

Melio

Bill payments for small businesses by bank transfer or card, syncing with your accounting tools. Card payments carry a flat 2.9%, which only pays off when you are deliberately floating cash.

What it costs

Free (Go, 5 free ACH/mo); Core $20/mo billed annually ($25 monthly). Read on Melio’s own pricing page on 2026-08-17; check the live page before you buy.

The free plan is genuinely useful for low bill volume, but the value case narrows as you scale into paid tiers — and the 2.9% card fee only makes sense when you're deliberately floating cash on a card for rewards or timing.

Who it suits

Small businesses that want to pay bills by bank transfer or card and get paid, starting free with no subscription.

Who should skip it

Skip it if you already run AP through robust accounting software with bill-pay built in — a second tool adds little.

Getting started

Getting started means creating an account with your business details (legal name, industry, address) and personal details, then connecting a way to pay — a bank account verified instantly through Plaid or via micro-deposits, or a card. Vendors get added the same way, with either their bank details for direct deposit or just a mailing address for a check. The first real payment happens from the Pay dashboard: start a new payment, enter the vendor, bill amount, invoice details and due date, then choose how and when the vendor gets paid.

TaxCycle

Professional tax preparation and CRA e-filing for Canadian accountants and bookkeepers, sold per module and per user, with bundles for the full suite. Canada-only, and not a consumer filing app.

What it costs

From CAD 618 a year (TaxCycle T1, one user, before tax; extra users CAD 238 each). Read on TaxCycle’s own pricing page on 2026-09-22; check the live page before you buy.

This is professional preparer software priced for firms, not a consumer filing app: the cost only makes sense if you're preparing returns for clients at volume. The suites start at about CAD $1,600 a year for one user and each extra seat adds several hundred dollars, so a two- or three-person practice should budget the suite price plus seats, plus GST or HST, before comparing it with per-return alternatives.

Who it suits

Canadian accountants and bookkeepers who prepare and e-file client returns and want fast, checked, CRA-integrated professional tax software.

Who should skip it

Skip it if you're an individual filing your own personal return (consumer software is far cheaper), or you're outside Canada.

Getting started

Price it per return before you judge any figure. At the regular $1,680, a practice preparing 200 T1s pays about $8.40 a return, while the same figure across 50 returns is $33.60. That division tells you far more than the annual number does, and it is the honest test of whether your book of clients supports professional preparer software at all.
